# Playing

Open the server's page in Chrome. Make a room, or join one from the list or with its code. The host picks the game, Black Ops, Black Ops II or Black Ops III, then a map of it. The others can suggest a map with a click on it. When everyone has pressed Ready, the game starts.

The page offers only the games the server hosts, and a server of one game has nothing to pick. The list of rooms says each room's game, and each game has its own leaderboards.

## Black Ops

Black Ops runs in the browser tab.

- **Browser:** Chrome, or another browser with WebGPU.
- **Game files:** you need your own copy of Black Ops. Open the menu under your name, and under Black Ops game folder choose the game's install folder. Each map then has an Import button. The browser keeps the imported files, and nothing from the folder is sent to the server. If the host has turned on downloads, the button says Download and no copy is needed.
- **Settings:** Black Ops settings, under Ready in a Black Ops room and under Play in Play local, opens the game's graphics, sound and controls. Your next game in this browser starts with them. In a game, the game's own menu has them.
- **Mouse and full screen:** a click captures the mouse and goes full screen. Esc opens the game's menu. Hold Esc or press F11 to leave full screen.
- **Gamepads:** Xbox and PlayStation controllers work. Press any button to turn the gamepad on. Start opens the settings where the page has them.
- **Play local:** Go, under Play local in the lobby, plays a map alone in the browser, on no game of the server's. It also works offline, without the server, for maps you have imported.
- **Dropped from a game:** rejoin within 5 minutes to get your character back.

## Black Ops II

Black Ops II runs on your Windows PC, on [Plutonium](https://plutonium.pw/). The Zombies Launcher starts it for you and joins your room's game. A Mac with Apple Silicon can play too: see [On a Mac](#on-a-mac).

- **Game files:** Black Ops II installed from Steam, or a copy of it anywhere on the PC. If the launcher doesn't find it, it asks: choose Choose folder… and pick the game's folder, the one with `t6zm.exe` in it. If the host has turned on downloads, the launcher can download the files instead (about 9 GB), into its own folder or one you choose.
- **Modes:** the host picks how the room plays in the room's settings, under Mode: Co-op, Grief or Turned. The maps below are those of the mode, and each map's card says its mode after its name. In Co-op, everyone plays together against the zombies, on a map's story or on Survival. In Grief (Bus Depot, Town, Farm, Cell Block, Borough), two teams survive the same rounds, and a team wins once every player of the other is down and it lives to the end of that round. The room shows each player's team: the first half of the room is team 1. A Grief room can take up to 8 players, four in each team. In Turned (Diner, Borough), one player is human and everyone else a zombie: whoever kills the human takes the cure, and the player with the most points when time runs out wins. A room of Grief or Turned needs at least 2 players to make sense, and its games count for no leaderboard: the room's report says who won.
- **Map packs:** a map of a pack you don't have shows you as Missing map, and you can't be ready until the host picks another map. If the host has turned on downloads, the room's button says Download: the launcher then asks whether to download the server's game files (about 9 GB) and use them instead. Your own copy stays as it is, and Choose Black Ops II folder… in the launcher's menu goes back to it.
- **First time:** install the launcher with the link under the room's button. The link is there until the launcher has run from your browser, and again when Connect starts nothing. It downloads from GitHub. Windows does not know the program and asks whether to run it: choose More info, then Run anyway.
- **Each game:** if the room's button says Connect, press it. The launcher brings Plutonium up to date, checks the game files and stays in the tray. Then press Ready. When the room's game starts, the launcher starts your game and joins it. You don't need to install Plutonium or sign in to it.
- **Launch game:** the room has a Launch game button under its main button. It starts your game now, at its menu, where you can set its graphics, sound and controls before the room's game starts. You don't have to press it. A game that is open joins the room's game in seconds, where it otherwise starts when the room's game does. The button is grey until the launcher has your PC ready, and says Game running while your game is open.
- **The next game:** when a game ends, the launcher has your game leave it, and your game is back at its menu. Leave it open there. When the room's next game starts, your game joins it without starting again. The launcher restarts your game instead when Plutonium has been updated, or when you chose another game folder in its menu.
- **Updates:** the launcher updates itself from GitHub. If the room says that your launcher is too old, install the new one with the link there, then press Connect.
- **Plutonium:** the launcher keeps Plutonium in Plutonium's own folder, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Plutonium`, where Plutonium's own launcher (`plutonium.exe`, from its site) has it too. So Plutonium is not part of the Zombies Launcher: a PC that has Plutonium already keeps it, and Plutonium stays when the launcher is uninstalled.
- **Play local:** the lobby's Play local has Black Ops II too, behind a switch where the server hosts both games. The switch shows the game you last picked or were last in a room of, as a new room and the leaderboards do. Its button is Connect until the launcher has had the game ready on your PC, as in a room, and Launch game from then on: it starts the game on your PC through Plutonium, in LAN mode, and you pick a map in the game. It needs no room, and no Plutonium account. The launcher plays with the Plutonium the PC has, whatever its version, and gets Plutonium only when the PC has none yet. So once the PC has played, it plays with no internet. Where the browser can't reach the server either, its copy of the page still has the button: the game then starts from the game files the PC has.
- **Plutonium's online servers:** the launcher starts the game in LAN mode, which is offline and needs no Plutonium account. For Plutonium's online servers, download and run Plutonium's own launcher (`plutonium.exe`, from [its install guide](https://plutonium.pw/docs/install/)), sign in with a [free Plutonium account](https://forum.plutonium.pw/register), and at Black Ops II press Setup, choose your game folder, then press Play. Once the Zombies Launcher has started the game, it has filled in that folder for Plutonium's launcher, where Plutonium had none, and Setup is done already. Otherwise the Zombies Launcher's tray menu shows the folder it uses: where it downloaded the game, that is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Zombies Launcher\t6` unless you chose another, and you can paste that into the folder picker. Both launchers use the one copy of Plutonium (see Plutonium, above), so you can play either way. Play local has these steps too, under Black Ops II.
- **Join by hand:** check Join by hand under the room's button to join from your game's console instead, in a game that Plutonium started in LAN mode. The room shows the line to type there. The launcher then leaves your game alone, even when it still runs from an earlier game.
- **The launcher's icon in the tray:** its menu's first line says the launcher's version, as Windows' list of apps does. The menu shows which game folder the launcher uses. Choose Black Ops II folder… points it at another copy, from the game's next start. Delete downloaded Black Ops II files… frees the space of files the launcher downloaded.
- **Your colour:** the game shows your points in the colour of your ring in the room, while everyone listed before you in the room is in the game. For that, the games join in the room's order: yours may stay at Connecting for up to 30 seconds while it waits for the game of a player before you.
- **Same PC:** keep the room's page open on the PC you play on. The server matches your game to your page by address.

If something goes wrong:

- **The launcher doesn't find your Black Ops II, or uses the wrong copy:** click its icon in the tray, choose Choose Black Ops II folder… and pick the game's folder. A folder above it works too, such as a Steam library.
- **"Black Ops II can't run from that folder":** the folder's path has a character other than A to Z, such as é or я. Move the game to a folder such as `D:\Games\Black Ops II` and choose that one.
- **A file is missing (`d3dx9_43.dll`, `XINPUT1_3.dll`, `MSVCR100.dll`):** run `redist\DirectX\DXSETUP.exe` and `redist\vcredist_x86.exe` from the game's folder, once.
- **"Your game is outdated":** press Join the game again.
- **Antivirus removes Plutonium's files:** add an exception for `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Plutonium`.
- **Anything else:** the launcher's log is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Zombies Launcher\launcher.log`.

To remove the launcher, uninstall Zombies Launcher in Windows' Settings under Apps. It asks whether to delete the game files that it downloaded too, and says how large they are. Its copy of BOIII's program goes with it. Plutonium stays, and so does what BOIII keeps in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\boiii`: to remove those, delete their folders.

## Black Ops III

Black Ops III runs on your Windows PC, on [BOIII](https://github.com/Ezz-lol/boiii-free). The Zombies Launcher starts it for you and joins your room's game, as it does Black Ops II. What is said there of the launcher holds here too: installing it, Connect and Ready, Launch game, its updates, and its icon in the tray, whose menu has this game's folder as well. This is what differs.

- **Steam:** the PC needs Steam installed, whoever owns the game: BOIII starts nothing without it. Steam need not be running.
- **Game files:** Black Ops III installed from Steam, with the maps you want to play, or a copy of it anywhere on the PC. If the launcher doesn't find it, it asks: choose Choose folder… and pick the game's folder, the one with `BlackOps3.exe` in it. If the host has turned on downloads, the launcher can download the files instead, into its own folder or one you choose: 51 GB that every map needs, which has Shadows of Evil, the map that the game comes with, and then 1 to 7 GB for each other map you play, as you come to it.
- **Maps you don't have:** a map that your game folder lacks shows you as Missing map, and the room's button says Download where the host has turned on downloads. Where the launcher downloaded your game files, it gets the map. Where they are your own copy, it asks whether to download the server's files and use them instead: that is the whole game again, and your own copy stays as it is.
- **BOIII:** the launcher gets BOIII's program once, into its own folder, and starts it in your game's folder. BOIII then brings itself up to date each time it starts, and keeps its data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\boiii`, as it does when you start it yourself. Your game plays under your name in the room: the launcher writes it into BOIII's settings in the game's folder as the game starts, and it stays there. A game that you start with your own BOIII in that folder then plays under it too, until you set another name there. A game that is open keeps the name it started with, so when you change your name in the lobby with the game open, or join by hand under your own, the room's game shows you under your name in the room all the same: on the scoreboard, and wherever the game names you.
- **Each game:** the launcher starts your game at its menu, and has it join the room's game from there once both are up: you see the game start, sit at its first screen for a moment, and then load the map. Don't press anything meanwhile. With Launch game, your game is at its menu before the room's game starts, and you can be in its settings until then.
- **The next game:** when a game ends, the launcher has your game leave it, and your game is back at its menu. Leave it open there: it joins the room's next game from where it is, without starting again. A game that you joined by hand is told instead that the server disconnected, as the room's game quits: that is how every game ends there, and nothing is wrong.
- **Windows' firewall:** the first time BOIII starts, Windows may ask whether public and private networks may access it. Either answer does: your game connects to the server, and nothing connects to your game. Cancel is enough.
- **Both games:** Black Ops II and Black Ops III can be open at once. When your room goes over to the other game, or you launch the other one from Play local, the launcher leaves the game that is open as it is, and starts or joins the other. When you come back to the first, the launcher picks it up again where it is, and starts no second one. Close a game yourself when you are done with it.
- **Play local:** the lobby's Play local has Black Ops III too. Its button is Connect until the launcher has had the game ready on your PC, as in a room, and Launch game from then on, which starts the game at its menu: you pick a map in the game. Where the host has turned on downloads, Play local shows the game's maps. A map that your PC has says nothing more. Where the launcher downloaded your game files, a map that your PC lacks shows its size: click it to download it, also while the game runs. In your own copy of the game, such a map says Not installed, and the launcher downloads nothing into it. The browser remembers what your PC had when the launcher last looked.
- **Join by hand:** check Join by hand under the room's button to join from your own BOIII instead: put `boiii.exe` from [its releases](https://github.com/Ezz-lol/boiii-free/releases/latest) in the game's folder and start it. When the room's game is up, the room shows a line: open the console in your game and type it there, or copy it with the button at its end. It gives the room's password and connects to the server.
- **Same PC:** keep the room's page open on the PC you play on. The server matches your game to your page by address.
- **On a Mac:** Black Ops III doesn't run there.

## On a Mac

Black Ops II is a Windows game. On a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), the launcher runs it in Wine, with [plutonium-mac-aio](https://github.com/LuidgiAlt/plutonium-mac-aio). An Intel Mac can't play it. What is said above of the launcher holds on a Mac too, but for this:

- **First time:** install the launcher with the line under the room's button: paste it into Terminal. The ? by the line says what it does. It installs the launcher, in `Library/Application Support/Zombies Launcher` of your home folder, and what the game runs on: plutonium-mac-aio, in the folder `plutonium-mac-aio` of your home folder, with its Wine and Plutonium. A Mac that has plutonium-mac-aio there already keeps its own. That takes a few minutes, most of it downloads, and ends with "Zombies Launcher successfully installed." From then on the room's Connect button starts the launcher. The line fetches a script from GitHub, and you need the script for nothing more. A click on the line selects it. The room has no button that copies it, because a content blocker would warn of one.
- **Game files:** the Mac needs the files of the Windows game. Where the host has turned on downloads, choose Download when the launcher asks. Otherwise copy the game's folder from a Windows PC to the Mac, and run the script's `folder <the folder>` command in Terminal (below).
- **What macOS asks:** Chrome asks whether to open Zombies Launcher. When the server is on your own network, macOS asks whether Zombies Launcher may find devices on local networks: allow it, or the launcher can't reach the server. As the game starts, macOS asks whether it may use the microphone, which is for the game's voice chat and not required.
- **The game's window:** it opens behind the browser, so switch to it with Command+Tab. The first time it is a window: the game's options have full screen, and the game remembers it.
- **The launcher's icon:** it is in the menu bar, where a PC has the tray.
- **Updates:** the launcher updates itself, but not what the line installed around it: the script that runs it, and plutonium-mac-aio. When a new launcher needs those newer, or the server needs a newer launcher, the room says that your launcher needs an update. Paste the room's line into Terminal again.
- **Plutonium's online servers:** with the Zombies Launcher installed, run `~/plutonium-mac-aio/launch.sh` in Terminal. That is plutonium-mac-aio's own way to start Plutonium's own launcher, on the same copy of Plutonium that the Zombies Launcher plays with. Sign in with a [free Plutonium account](https://forum.plutonium.pw/register), and at Black Ops II press Setup, enter your game folder, then press Play. Once the Zombies Launcher has started the game, it has filled in that folder, and Setup is done already. Otherwise the folder is the one that the Zombies Launcher's menu in the menu bar shows, written as that dialog needs it. If a dialog first asks whether to quit Plutonium, choose Quit and restart: it means the Zombies Launcher, which a room's Connect starts again.
- **If something goes wrong:** the script has commands for Terminal, each after `bash ~/Library/Application\ Support/Zombies\ Launcher/zombies-launcher-mac.sh`. `log` shows the launcher's log and Wine's, and `stop` closes the launcher and the game.

To remove the launcher, open Zombies Launcher in the Applications folder of your home folder, and choose Uninstall. It asks first, and says how large the game files are that the launcher downloaded, which go with it. plutonium-mac-aio stays, with Plutonium, as Plutonium stays on a PC: to remove it too, delete the folder `plutonium-mac-aio` in your home folder. Nothing of the launcher's is in it. Without it the room's Connect starts nothing, until you paste the room's line into Terminal again, which downloads plutonium-mac-aio again: the launcher's settings and the game files that it downloaded are still there. Don't move the folder: Wine then starts no game.
